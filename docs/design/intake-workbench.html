    What customer value are we testing?

    Fewer routine human touches per correctly completed handoff, without hidden failures or missed urgent cases. Exception resolution and oversight effort count too. Fast inference alone is not success.

    Where Jev, Rust, and an LLM fit

    Jev is a candidate for bounded category, urgency, and review-required judgments. A structured-output LLM and deterministic rules must be compared on the same task. Rust validates results and applies routing policy. No provider is proven better by this mockup.

    Generation, coverage/payout decisions, and exact arithmetic are not Jev's role here. No fabricated confidence numbers or generated advice are displayed.
