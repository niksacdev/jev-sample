    Original customer submission

      Your agent team, one accountable journey

      Rue coordinates servicing, evidence, policy, loss assessment, resolution and payment agents. Each has a scoped responsibility and produces inspectable artifacts. These are proposed roles, not six deployed services.

      The value hypothesis is less customer chasing, less repeated evidence gathering and fewer routine human touches through claim resolution. Correct outcomes, disputes, exception effort and payment accuracy matter—not just completion speed.

      Where Jev, Rust, and an LLM fit

      Jev is a candidate for bounded category, urgency, and review-required judgments. A structured-output LLM and deterministic rules must be compared on the same task. Rust validates results and applies routing policy. No provider is proven better by this mockup.

      Generation, coverage/payout decisions, and exact arithmetic are not Jev's role here. No fabricated confidence numbers or generated advice are displayed.

      Questions for analyst design review

      Is the next action clear? Can you challenge a wrong suggestion? Does the assessment save reading time or add verification work? Which missing fields or queue names would prevent this from fitting your actual job?

      Routine actions run within predelegated policy. Exceptions pause, gain a named owner, and resume only after the required intervention. Real authority, thresholds, messaging permissions, and tool identity still need contract approval.
